# 00 — Product Brief

## 1. Product

**Name:** YapYep  
**Positioning:** ASEAN Student Adaptation Network  
**Platform:** Mobile-first web/PWA  
**Core audience:** ASEAN exchange and international university students  
**Secondary audience:** Local students, current exchange students, incoming students, returned exchange students

### One-line pitch

> YapYep helps exchange students understand what people mean, practice what to do next, and adapt to student life across ASEAN.

### Differentiating sentence

> Translation tools tell students what was said. YapYep helps them interpret likely intent and context, rehearse the next interaction, and learn from the real outcome.

## 2. Challenge fit

Track 2 asks how AI can help students overcome **language and cultural barriers** to **learn, communicate, and adapt** in international environments.

YapYep maps directly:

- **Learn:** Study Copilot + YapSim + feedback + adaptive practice.
- **Communicate:** YapLens + Tone Check + Reply Builder + realtime translation.
- **Adapt:** PairDNA + Country Passport + Explore + people/community + reflection.

## 3. Problem statement

Exchange students do not only struggle with literal translation. They repeatedly encounter situations where:
- they understand the words but not the intent;
- academic expectations are implicit;
- they are unsure whether a message sounds rude, indirect, confrontational, or optional;
- language uncertainty reduces participation;
- university/country support information is fragmented;
- local human help is not available at the exact moment it is needed.

### Product insight

**The problem is often not “What does this mean?” but “What do they mean, and what am I supposed to do next?”**

## 4. Market/strategic rationale

ASEAN is actively promoting a common higher-education space and stronger people-to-people/student mobility. Timor-Leste became ASEAN's 11th member on 26 Oct 2025, so the product must model 11 countries, not 10.

YapYep therefore uses:
- 11 ASEAN countries,
- 110 directional home→host pairs,
- user-specific communication profile,
- city/university context,
- scenario context,
- local verification.

## 5. Users

### Primary persona
A university student moving between ASEAN countries for exchange/short-term/full international study.

Needs:
- prepare before arrival,
- handle university communication,
- understand local/contextual language,
- make friends,
- solve daily-life setup,
- learn without feeling dependent on a generic chatbot.

### Secondary personas
- Local student: can answer/verify context and meet incoming students.
- Current exchange student: can share current lived experience.
- Incoming student: wants pre-arrival preparation and connections.
- Returned student: can give high-value bridge advice.

## 6. Jobs to be done

When I receive a confusing message, help me understand the likely meaning and decide how to respond.

When I need to speak to a professor/team/local person, let me practice before the real interaction.

When I arrive in a new ASEAN country, show only the student-relevant information I need now, backed by current sources.

When I feel isolated or uncertain, connect me with the most contextually relevant person rather than a random language partner.

When I keep making the same communication mistake, detect it and turn it into tomorrow's practice.

## 7. Product loop

`PREPARE → ARRIVE → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE → INTERACT → REFLECT → ADAPT`

Closed-loop behavior:
1. user encounters problem,
2. YapLens interprets,
3. user rehearses in YapSim,
4. user interacts with a real person,
5. user reflects,
6. skill/profile updates,
7. next Today task adapts.

## 8. Product boundaries

### In scope
- intercultural communication
- academic/student communication
- local language/context
- preparation and arrival guidance
- student-relevant daily systems
- student connections
- student-specific exploration
- measurable adaptation/learning

### Out of core
- scholarship discovery
- job marketplace
- recruitment
- tourism booking
- visa filing automation
- immigration legal advice
- full banking transaction product
- generic social media feed
- general-purpose homework solver

Administrative/system information may be included as sourced guidance, not as professional legal/financial advice.

## 9. Core moat hypothesis

**ASEAN Student Context Graph**

`CountryDNA × UniversityDNA × PairDNA × MyDNA × Interaction history × Local verification`

The long-term defensibility is not the chat interface; it is accumulated structured, directional, source-backed student adaptation context plus verified real-user feedback.

## 10. North-star metric

**Successful intercultural interactions resolved or practiced per active student.**

Supporting:
- completed scenarios,
- retry score improvement,
- weekly active learners,
- locally verified guidance coverage,
- user-reported confidence after real interactions.
